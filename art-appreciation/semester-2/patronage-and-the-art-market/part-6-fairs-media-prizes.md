# Part 6: Fairs, Media, Prizes, and the Visibility Economy

## Overview

This part treats the contemporary art world as an attention system. Visibility is not a byproduct of making good work. It is a currency that circulates through specific structures: fairs concentrate access, media stabilizes reputations, and prizes accelerate legitimacy. Students learn how these systems work, who controls them, and what they cost the artists who move through them.

---

## Goals and Objectives

**By the end of the week, students should be able to:**
- Explain what art fairs do structurally: distribution, access, and sorting
- Describe how media and prizes function as certification mechanisms
- Identify how visibility pressures shape artistic labor and career strategy
- Map the flow from attention to legitimacy to price using documented evidence
- Name the labor costs of professional maintenance as a distinct kind of work
