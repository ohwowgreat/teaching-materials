# Lesson 2: Shared Lunch Discussion

**Theme:** Food and Ethics
**Focus:** Eating Together and Talking about the Film

## Overview

Today we come together as one group to share lunch and talk about the film *Eat Drink Man Woman*. Our goal is to experience what John Berger calls *"the sharing of bread,"* and to think about how this act itself can be ethical. As we eat, we will notice how food creates dialogue, connection, and care both on screen and at our own table.

## Discussion Prompts

- How does cooking in the film act as a way of speaking or showing love?
- What moments in *Eat Drink Man Woman* reveal conflict, duty, or change through food?
- Can pleasure in eating be moral?
- How does our own shared meal reflect Berger's idea of food as a pledge that all will live?
