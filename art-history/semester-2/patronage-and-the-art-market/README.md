# Patronage and the Art Market

This unit studies how art gains value through systems: patronage, institutions, markets, media, and finance. It begins with the history of patronage, including the Catholic Church and Baroque narrative control, then moves into the modern art market. The final week is a professional workshop ending in a real-world submission.
