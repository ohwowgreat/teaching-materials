# Part 7: 山寨 (Shanzhai)

## Overview

This part uses shanzhai as a framework for understanding how copying, versioning, and circulation shape legitimacy in contemporary culture. Shanzhai names a Chinese practice of reproduction and iteration that is not simply imitation: it is a form of creative engagement with existing forms, producing variants that circulate through different networks and accumulate their own authority.

Students connect this to the attention economy, where visibility and recognition operate as currency, and to NFTs, where scarcity is designed through platforms and metadata. Contemporary Chinese artists are the primary case studies.

This is the summative synthesis checkpoint before professional practice. The case study assignment asks students to bring together the full vocabulary of the unit: shanzhai logic, circulation mechanisms, legitimacy proof chains, and at least one mechanism from the attention economy or NFT systems.

---

## Goals and Objectives

**By the end of the week, students should be able to:**
- Use shanzhai to describe versioning, remix, and iteration as productive cultural logic rather than simple copying
- Identify the circulation nodes that produce legitimacy for a specific artist or work
- Explain how the attention economy converts visibility into reputational currency
- Describe how NFTs design scarcity through platform infrastructure and metadata
- Produce a case study argument that is specific, evidence-based, and uses precise terminology

## Reading
