# Capstone: Atlas Book Contribution

## Description

This is your contribution to the class atlas book. The book is built collectively from work produced across both semesters. Your job is to bring something in that belongs in it.

Submit 2 to 5 items. Each item can be an artwork, a concept, a quote, a film still, a data point, a screenshot, a page from a catalogue, or any other media that has mattered to you in this course. For each item, write one or two sentences explaining why it belongs.

There is no required theme. You are not being asked to build an argument or write an essay. You are being asked to make a deliberate selection and say something precise about it.

---

## Objectives

- Select material with intention and say something specific about why it belongs
- Connect your choices to ideas, artworks, or frameworks from the course
- Demonstrate the kind of constellation thinking the course has been building toward

---

## Directions

1. Choose 2 to 5 items. An item can be an artwork, a quote, a concept, a moving image, a document, a platform screenshot, or any media relevant to the course.
2. For each item, write one or two sentences: what it is, and why it belongs in this book.
3. Submit everything as a single file. Images should be clearly labeled. Text should be readable.

---

## Rubric

| Level | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Excellent** | Every item is chosen with clear intention. The reasoning is specific: it names something precise about the item and connects it to a course idea, artwork, or argument. The selection as a whole shows a distinct point of view. |
| **Strong** | Items are well chosen and the reasoning is mostly clear. Connections to the course are present but one or two explanations stay general rather than specific. |
| **Developing** | Items are recognizable as course-related but the reasoning is thin or vague. It is not clear why this item rather than another. |
| **Beginning** | Items are submitted without meaningful reasoning, or the reasoning does not connect to anything from the course. |

---

## Submission

Due at the end of the year. Single file. Any format that preserves image quality and is easy to read.
